About SwiftRide

Your trusted partner for smart, reliable transport booking.

Our Mission

We built SwiftRide to make daily travel simple. Whether it's a quick city cab, an intercity van, or a scheduled airport pickup, our goal is to connect riders with reliable drivers in seconds.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, SwiftRide started as a student project and grew into a demo platform showcasing how modern transport booking should feel — fast, transparent, and friendly.
